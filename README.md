## 📈 3. MYPE Productivity and ICT Analysis (Power BI - Academic Project)

Business intelligence project focused on analyzing productivity, ICT adoption, operational performance, commercial digitalization, and strategic improvement opportunities in micro and small enterprises (MYPEs).

The project was developed using survey data from RELAYN 2026. The dataset was cleaned and transformed in Power Query, removing sensitive personal information and keeping only relevant analytical variables. Several calculated indexes were created to summarize Likert-scale questions into measurable business indicators.

### 📌 Project Objective:

To analyze how ICT adoption, productivity practices, operational control, resource management, and business results are related within MYPEs using interactive Power BI dashboards.

### 📂 Power BI File:

[Download Power BI Project File](MYPES.pbix)

### 🧹 Data Preparation:

- Cleaned and filtered valid survey responses
- Removed personal and sensitive business information
- Converted Likert-scale answers into numerical values
- Created analytical variables such as business sector, company size, worker ranges, ICT maturity level, and productivity level
- Created business indexes for ICT, planning, control, operational productivity, resource management, commercial digitalization, and general productivity-ICT performance
- Built auxiliary tables for filtering and dashboard interaction
- Created calculated measures using DAX for KPIs, percentages, averages, and strategic classifications

### 📊 Dashboards Developed:

#### 1. ICT Digitalization Diagnosis

Dashboard focused on analyzing the level of digital adoption among MYPEs.

**Main analysis:**
- ICT adoption index
- ICT maturity level distribution
- ICT components analysis
- Digitalization challenges
- ICT comparison by business sector

**Key indicators:**
- Average ICT index
- Companies with high ICT maturity
- Percentage of companies with high ICT maturity
- Commercial digital index
- General productivity and ICT index

#### 2. Operational Productivity and Business Control

Dashboard focused on analyzing internal productivity, planning, control, and resource management.

**Main analysis:**
- Operational productivity index
- Planning index
- Control index
- Resource management index
- Productivity level distribution
- Main operational challenges

**Key indicators:**
- Average operational productivity
- Average planning score
- Average control score
- Average resource management score
- Companies with high productivity
- Percentage of companies with high productivity

#### 3. ICT, Productivity, and Business Results

Dashboard focused on comparing ICT and productivity indicators with business results such as sales and profits.

**Main analysis:**
- Relationship between ICT adoption and sales behavior
- Productivity and ICT analysis by profit changes
- Sales results by ICT maturity level
- Comparison of ICT and productivity by business result groups

**Key indicators:**
- Total valid surveys
- Average ICT index
- General productivity and ICT index
- Percentage of companies with increased sales
- Percentage of companies with increased profits

#### 4. Strategic Opportunities Map

Dashboard focused on identifying strategic improvement opportunities based on ICT maturity and productivity levels.

**Main analysis:**
- Strategic classification based on ICT maturity and productivity level
- Identification of companies with improvement opportunities
- Strategic distribution by business sector
- Main challenges among companies with low ICT or low productivity performance

**Key indicators:**
- Percentage of companies in strategic strength
- Percentage of companies with improvement opportunities
- Average ICT index
- General productivity and ICT index
- Total companies analyzed

### 📌 Key Insights:

- The average ICT index was **3.88 / 5**, showing a medium-high level of digital adoption.
- The general productivity and ICT index reached **4.25 / 5**, indicating strong productivity-related practices.
- **47.05%** of companies showed high ICT maturity.
- **56.21%** of companies showed high productivity levels.
- **74.54%** of companies reported increased sales.
- **68.84%** of companies reported increased profits.
- A significant group of companies showed both high productivity and high ICT maturity, representing strategic strengths.
- The main improvement areas were related to investment limitations, unclear goals, workflow issues, lack of follow-up, poor coordination, and limited digital tools.
